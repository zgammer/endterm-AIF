# Промпты для иллюстраций

Размеры: иллюстрации 1200×800, PNG или WebP. Положите файлы в `img/` с указанными именами.

## Общий стиль (в начало каждого промпта)

> Flat 2D medieval illuminated-manuscript illustration in the style of a fairy-tale storybook. Gouache and ink on aged cream parchment, thin dark-brown outlines, soft hand-painted texture, limited palette of deep navy blue, brick red, forest green, warm ochre and gold-leaf accents. Heraldic folk-art composition with clean, readable silhouettes. Whimsical, warm, slightly humorous mood. No text, no letters, no numbers, no 3D rendering, no photorealism, no glossy gradients, no recognizable copyrighted characters or film stills.

## Общие

**cover.png** (900×1200)
> Front cover of an old leather-bound storybook, dark brown leather texture with worn edges. A tall, empty rectangular panel in the center, framed by a gold ornamental border with small vines and leaves. Two heraldic beasts (a lion and a griffin) sit at the top and bottom corners, painted in navy and red. Small gold stars scattered around the frame. Leave the center panel clear for a title. Soft vignette lighting from above.

**endpaper.png** (800×800)
> Inside cover endpaper of a fairy-tale book. A symmetrical red and blue diamond pattern in the center, surrounded by stylized oak leaves and two small birds facing each other. Corners decorated with tiny gold quatrefoils. Aged cream paper with slight foxing and faint fibers. Calm, balanced, decorative composition with plenty of negative space at the edges.

**parchment.jpg** (1024×1024, бесшовная)
> Seamless texture of aged parchment paper, warm cream to light tan color. Subtle uneven fibers, a few faint water stains, tiny darker spots near the edges, and soft wear. No drawings, no text, no lines. Even lighting so the texture can repeat smoothly when tiled.

**table-wood.jpg** (1920×1080)
> Top-down view of a dark, aged wooden table with visible grain and small scratches. Warm, dim candlelight from the upper left, falling off into deep shadow toward the corners. Surface is empty and slightly out of focus, so text and pages can sit on top of it. Muted brown tones with a faint golden highlight.

## Глава I: поиск пути

**ch1-01-quest.png**
> A brave knight in shining armor stands at the gates of a small stone castle on the left side of the image. A winding dirt road leads from his feet across rolling green hills toward a distant blue tower on the right horizon. Small trees dot the path. Two white birds fly in the sky. The knight holds a banner and points forward with his free hand. The road is the visual focus, so keep it clear and easy to follow.

**ch1-02-fringe.png**
> A large, stylized tree whose branches are shaped like roads and paths. At the base of the trunk, a small knight holds a lantern whose golden light illuminates the nearest branches. Some branches glow softly in gold, while the others fade into the parchment. Each branch ends in a tiny round node, suggesting a frontier of unexplored options. Decorative and symmetric.

**ch1-03-astar.png**
> A knight with a brass spyglass held to one eye, gazing at a faraway castle on a hill. Beside him stands a wooden signpost with three arrows pointing in different directions, each marked only with small symbols (no letters). A compass lies on the ground at his feet, its needle pointing toward the castle. Warm, confident mood, with the castle as the clear goal.

## Глава II: локальный поиск

**ch2-01-landscape.png**
> A side-view landscape of rolling hills forming a jagged skyline. A small, low peak sits near the viewer with a little knight standing on top, holding a flag. Farther back, a much taller mountain rises on the right, with a golden star above its summit. Between the two peaks lies a dip in the land. Clean, readable silhouettes with a layered atmospheric haze.

**ch2-02-restarts.png**
> Three knights, each with a different colored banner (red, green, and blue), start from three separate hilltops across the scene. Two of the knights have reached smaller peaks, and the third has climbed to the tallest mountain in the background. Thin dotted paths show each knight's route. Lively, balanced composition.

**ch2-03-annealing.png**
> A blacksmith in a leather apron stands at a glowing forge, lifting a red-hot piece of metal with tongs toward an anvil. Sparks fly upward in gold and orange. On the anvil, the metal glows bright yellow at one end and deep red at the other, showing the cooling process. A bucket of water sits nearby. Warm, dynamic composition with strong contrast between the fire and the dark background.

## Глава III: вероятность и байесовские сети

**ch3-01-alarm.png**
> A stone bell tower on a hill, with a large bronze bell visibly ringing. A thin wisp of smoke rises from a distant chimney, while a sly red fox peeks from behind a bush on the right with a curious look. Villagers in the lower corner look up toward the bell, some worried, some unbothered. Conveys the idea that an alarm does not always mean fire.

**ch3-02-bayes.png**
> A wise owl scholar in a tiny pointed hat sits on a stack of old books, holding a balance scale. On the left pan lies a small heavy stone (the old belief), on the right pan a glowing feather (new evidence), and the scale tips gently. A scroll and a quill lie nearby. Calm, scholarly mood.

**ch3-03-network.png**
> Three stations linked by a flowing ribbon from left to right: a small campfire, a puffy cloud of smoke, and a bronze alarm bell on a post. Curling vines with arrowheads between them point left to right. A tiny knight inspects the bell and looks back toward the fire, suggesting reasoning backwards from effect to cause.

## Глава IV: скрытые марковские модели

**ch4-01-hidden.png**
> A dark stone castle wrapped in mist. Only a silhouette of a tall figure is visible behind a pale curtain in one window, and a small lantern glows in a lower window. On the muddy path below, faint footprints lead away from the castle. The scene suggests that a hidden presence is inferred from clues, not seen directly.

**ch4-02-clues.png**
> Two rows. The upper row is a line of dim arched windows, each holding a different shadowy silhouette (calm, restless, ailing) in faded blue. The lower row is a line of hanging lanterns glowing in different colors (green, amber, red). Thin golden threads link each window to the lantern below it, suggesting hidden states that emit visible signals.

**ch4-03-path.png**
> A knight walking across a river on a line of flat stepping stones through drifting fog. A few stones glow gold and form the single best path, while the others are dim. Small arrows carved into the stones point forward. Moonlit, calm mood.

## Главы V–VII (на будущее)

**ch5-decisions.png**
> A knight stands before an old wooden balance scale. On one pan lies a heavy sack of gold coins, and on the other a shining round shield. The scale is tipping slightly toward the shield. A small scroll with a simple table of numbers (drawn as abstract lines, no legible text) rests on a stone pedestal beside him. Thoughtful, measured mood.

**ch6-clusters.png**
> A medieval market square seen from above. Villagers gather in three distinct groups, each beneath a flag of a different color, while a few lone figures stand apart near the edge of the square. Stalls and cobblestones frame the scene. The groups are clearly separated but the layout feels organic and natural.

**ch7-rl.png**
> A knight walks through a hedge maze in a sunlit garden. Behind him, small golden lanterns mark the path he has already taken. Ahead, at the very center of the maze, a wooden treasure chest overflows with gold coins. Tall green hedges form clear walls; a few paths are dimly lit, showing that some routes are better explored than others.
